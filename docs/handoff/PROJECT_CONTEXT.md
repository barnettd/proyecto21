# PROYECTO 21 — Project Context

## Project Summary
PROYECTO 21 / P.21 is a private 20+1 day anniversary experience built around music.

- D0: 15 Sep 2026 — prologue/package
- D1: 16 Sep 2026
- D20: 5 Oct 2026
- D21: 6 Oct 2026 — 20th anniversary / Track 21 / continuation into P21+

The project combines physical objects, printed pieces, music exchange, email, a private web experience, children/friends/family contributions, deferred callbacks, and one original AI-assisted song created from material gathered during the project.

The goal is not a scavenger hunt or questionnaire series. It should feel like a coherent, mysterious, intimate project that listens, remembers, and evolves.

## Creative Intent
Desired:
- mystery
- complicity
- surprise
- humor
- intimacy
- real participation

Avoid:
- romantic clichés
- excessive sentimentality
- startup/tech tone
- hacker aesthetics
- generic gamification
- repetitive forms
- forcing every day to be digital

Music is the main connective tissue.

Operating rule:
> Whenever practical, every day includes at least one song she gives and one song she receives.

D0 is an intentional exception.

## Narrative Logic
P21 should:
1. appear unexpectedly;
2. ask for small but genuine participation;
3. remember previous inputs;
4. reuse earlier material later;
5. gradually become more personal;
6. culminate in the playlist + Track 21;
7. continue as Proyecto 21+.

Known callbacks:
- D0 key → D17 box
- D2 Shower Songs → D13 Morning Warm
- D6 lyric/line → potential Track 21 inspiration
- D10 Guilty Pleasure → possible later humorous callback
- D20 future-facing track → D21 / P21+

## Digital Product Philosophy
The digital system is support infrastructure, not the project itself.

It must work alongside:
- physical activations
- email
- people
- real-world moments

At any moment the participant should see only:
- persistent P21 identity
- current active experience
- current action required

No calendar.
No history.
No future preview.
No traditional navigation.

- be revealed on D21 together with the accumulated playlist.
